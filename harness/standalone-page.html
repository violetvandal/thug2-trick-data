Tap Variants

Not separately assignable, these fire when you tap the button again during a trick. A few chain a third time: Kickflip → Double Kickflip → Triple Kickflip. The ones tagged with a game mode aren't normal tricks at all: in Firefight, holding the fireball powerup turns your flip trick into a shot, and the name that pops up scales with how many times you tapped. They only ever appear in that mode.

Grinds

direction + grind button

Grinds are directional, they're just not listed in the Edit Tricks menu, which is why they're missing from most trick lists. Hold a direction as you press the grind button and it picks the family below. Whether you get the frontside, backside or 180 version is decided by your stance and approach angle, so each family covers several named variants.

Manuals

balance, then branch

Manuals work like grinds: they aren't in the Edit Tricks menu either. You tap two directions to get into one, then press pairs of face buttons while balancing to branch into everything else. Branches chain, so you can run Casper into Truckstand into Pogo without ever touching the ground.
